# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participation in this project is governed by the standards in this document, regardless of whether participation occurs in code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, accept feedback in good faith, protect personal and confidential information, and prioritize the safety and inclusion of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, deliberate disruption, or other conduct that a reasonable person would consider hostile or unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when this policy is violated.
